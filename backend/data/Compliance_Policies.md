# XYZ Company - Compliance Policies

**Document set:** COM-001, COM-002, COM-003

This document consolidates XYZ Company's current compliance policies covering data privacy, anti-bribery and gifts, and whistleblower protection. Each section keeps its original policy ID and version.

---

## COM-001: Data Privacy and Personal Data Protection Policy

**Document ID:** COM-001
**Version:** 2.2
**Effective date:** 15 May 2025
**Owner:** Data Protection Officer (DPO)
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and third parties processing personal data for XYZ Company

### 1. Definitions

Personal data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable person, including names, email addresses, identification numbers, location data, and online identifiers.

Special category data includes health data, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, genetic and biometric data, and data concerning sexual orientation.

### 2. Principles

Personal data must be:

- processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently;
- collected for specified purposes and not used for incompatible purposes;
- limited to what is necessary;
- accurate and kept up to date;
- retained no longer than necessary;
- protected by appropriate security measures.

### 3. Lawful basis and special category data

3.1 Every processing activity must have a documented lawful basis and be recorded in the Record of Processing Activities (RoPA).

3.2 Special category data may only be processed where an additional condition applies and has been approved by the DPO.

### 4. Retention

4.1 Customer account data is retained for 7 years after account closure.

4.2 Employee records are retained for 6 years after the end of employment.

4.3 Job applicant data for unsuccessful candidates is deleted after 12 months unless the candidate has consented to a longer period.

4.4 Retention periods may be extended only with DPO approval and a documented legal reason.

### 5. Data subject rights

5.1 Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data.

5.2 Requests must be acknowledged within 5 business days and fulfilled within 30 calendar days. Complex requests may be extended by up to a further 60 days, with the individual informed of the reason.

### 6. Personal data breaches

6.1 Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the DPO within 24 hours of discovery at dpo@example.com or through the Security Incident Portal.

6.2 Where a breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals, the DPO will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of XYZ Company becoming aware of it.

6.3 Affected individuals are notified without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

### 7. International transfers

7.1 Personal data may not be transferred outside the European Economic Area unless an approved transfer mechanism is in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an adequacy decision.

7.2 Transfer impact assessments must be completed before any new transfer is started.

### 8. Third parties

All vendors that process personal data must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before access is granted. Due diligence must be completed as part of onboarding (see FIN-003 section 4.2).

### 9. Training

All staff must complete data protection training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter.

## COM-002: Anti-Bribery, Corruption, Gifts and Hospitality Policy

**Document ID:** COM-002
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective date:** 1 February 2026
**Owner:** Compliance
**Applies to:** All employees, directors, contractors, and agents acting for XYZ Company

### 1. Zero tolerance

XYZ Company prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms, whether direct or through third parties. Facilitation payments, which are small payments to speed up routine government actions, are not permitted in any country.

### 2. Gifts

2.1 Employees may give or accept gifts that are modest, infrequent, and consistent with local custom.

2.2 The maximum value of a single gift is $100, whether given or received.

2.3 The aggregate value of gifts exchanged with any one external organisation must not exceed $300 in a calendar year.

2.4 All gifts given or received with a value of $25 or more must be recorded in the Gifts Register within 5 business days.

2.5 Cash and cash equivalents, including gift cards and vouchers, may never be given or accepted, regardless of value.

### 3. Hospitality

3.1 Business meals, entertainment, and events are permitted when they have a legitimate business purpose and are reasonable in value.

3.2 Hospitality valued above $250 per person requires approval from the employee's manager and the Compliance team in advance.

3.3 Hospitality must never be offered to influence a decision, and must not be offered at a time when a related procurement or regulatory decision is pending.

### 4. Public officials

4.1 Any gift, hospitality, or payment to a public official, including officials of state-owned enterprises, requires prior approval from Compliance regardless of value.

4.2 Employees must not offer anything of value to a public official to obtain or retain business or any improper advantage.

### 5. Reporting

5.1 Any offer of an improper payment or gift must be declined and reported to Compliance within 1 business day.

5.2 Concerns may also be raised through the channels in COM-003.

### 6. Training and certification

All employees must complete anti-bribery training by 31 December each year. Employees must certify annually that they have read and complied with this policy. Failure to complete training within the deadline is escalated to the employee's manager and may result in disciplinary action.

### 7. Consequences of breach

Breaches of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal, and may be referred to law enforcement authorities.

## COM-003: Speak Up and Whistleblower Protection Policy

**Document ID:** COM-003
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective date:** 1 October 2025
**Owner:** Compliance and Legal
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, former employees, and suppliers

### 1. Purpose

XYZ Company encourages everyone who works with us to raise concerns about suspected wrongdoing, including fraud, bribery, safety risks, data breaches, harassment, and breaches of law or company policy.

### 2. Reporting channels

Concerns may be raised through any of the following:

- The Ethics Hotline, operated by an independent third party and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- The Compliance team at compliance@example.com.
- The employee's line manager, who must pass the concern to Compliance within 1 business day.
- A senior leader designated as a Speak Up Champion.

### 3. Anonymous reporting

Concerns may be raised anonymously through the Ethics Hotline. Anonymous reporters are encouraged to provide enough detail for an investigation to proceed and to use the hotline's secure follow-up feature.

### 4. Handling and timescales

4.1 Every concern is acknowledged within 2 business days of receipt.

4.2 An initial assessment begins within 10 business days, to decide whether a formal investigation is needed.

4.3 Investigations aim to conclude within 90 days. Reporters are informed of progress and outcome where the investigation allows.

4.4 Concerns involving senior leaders or the Compliance team are referred to the Audit Committee.

### 5. Protection from retaliation

5.1 XYZ Company prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, even if the concern is not substantiated.

5.2 Retaliation includes dismissal, demotion, reduction in pay, exclusion, harassment, or any other detriment. Retaliation is a serious disciplinary offence.

5.3 Knowingly false allegations made in bad faith are a breach of this policy.

### 6. Confidentiality

Reporter identities are kept confidential and shared only on a need-to-know basis. Identity may be disclosed where required by law.

### 7. External reporting

Nothing in this policy prevents an individual from reporting to a regulator or other authority where the law allows or requires it.
