# XYZ Company - Finance Policies

**Document set:** FIN-001, FIN-002, FIN-003

This document consolidates XYZ Company's current finance policies covering expenses, business travel, and procurement. Each section keeps its original policy ID and version.

---

## FIN-001: Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-001
**Version:** 4.1
**Effective date:** 1 July 2025
**Owner:** Finance Operations
**Applies to:** All employees and contractors submitting expense claims

### 1. Scope

This policy covers out-of-pocket business expenses incurred by employees and contractors. Travel bookings made through the Travel Portal are governed by FIN-002. Purchases of goods or services for the company are governed by FIN-003.

### 2. Submission

2.1 All expense claims must be submitted in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.

2.2 Claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense date will not be reimbursed, regardless of the reason for delay.

2.3 Each claim must include a clear business purpose.

### 3. Receipts

3.1 An itemised receipt is required for every expense above $25.

3.2 Where a receipt is lost, a signed declaration may be accepted for amounts up to $75 per item, with a limit of two such declarations per quarter.

### 4. Approval thresholds

Claims are approved according to their total value:

| Claim value | Required approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Line manager |
| $500.01 to $5,000 | Line manager and Finance Business Partner |
| Above $5,000 | Line manager, Finance Business Partner, and a CFO delegate |

Approvers may not approve their own claims. Claims for a manager's own expenses are approved by their own manager.

### 5. Payment

5.1 Approved claims are reimbursed to the employee's registered bank account within 10 business days of final approval.

5.2 Reimbursement is made in the currency of the claim, converted at the rate published by Treasury on the date of the expense.

### 6. Non-reimbursable items

The following are not reimbursed:

- Personal entertainment, including films, personal streaming subscriptions, and personal gym memberships.
- Alcohol, except where it forms part of approved client entertainment (see COM-002 for hospitality limits).
- Fines, penalties, and traffic violations.
- Personal travel insurance purchased for leisure trips.
- Commuting costs between home and the employee's regular office hub.

### 7. Per diem meal allowances

Where a meal is not included in an approved trip, the following daily per diem limits apply, as set out in FIN-002: $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day for international travel.

## FIN-002: Business Travel Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-002
**Version:** 3.2
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Finance Operations and Travel Office
**Applies to:** All employees travelling on company business

### 1. Booking

1.1 All flights, hotels, and rail journeys must be booked through the Travel Portal.

1.2 Trips should be booked at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made less than 7 days before departure require Director approval.

1.3 Bookings made outside the portal are reimbursable only with prior written approval from the Travel Office.

### 2. Air travel

2.1 Flights of up to 6 hours in duration must be booked in economy class.

2.2 Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class is permitted for flights longer than 6 hours only for employees at Vice President level and above, or with written approval from the CFO.

2.3 Employees must book the lowest logical fare available on the portal, subject to reasonable schedule constraints.

### 3. Rail

Rail travel is booked in standard class. First class rail requires CFO approval.

### 4. Accommodation

4.1 The standard nightly hotel cap is $300 for most destinations.

4.2 The following designated high-cost cities have a nightly cap of $450: London, New York, San Francisco, Zurich, Singapore, and Tokyo.

4.3 Hotel stays exceeding the cap require pre-approval from the employee's Finance Business Partner. The excess amount is not reimbursable without that approval.

### 5. Meals and incidentals

5.1 Where meals are not provided, a per diem of $75 per day applies for domestic travel within the employee's home country.

5.2 A per diem of $100 per day applies for international travel.

5.3 Per diem claims do not require itemised receipts but must be claimed in ExpenseHub under the "Per diem" category. Per diem is not payable for days on which the employee is not travelling overnight, unless the trip is a same-day business trip of more than 8 hours.

### 6. Ground transport

Taxis and ride-share services are reimbursable for business travel. Trips above $100 require a note explaining the reason for using a premium option or the reason no public transport option was suitable.

### 7. Cancellations

Non-refundable fares cancelled for reasons within the employee's control are not reimbursed. Cancellations caused by company business changes or documented illness are reimbursed in full.

### 8. Policy compliance

Travel that does not comply with this policy will be reviewed by the employee's Finance Business Partner. Repeated non-compliance may result in restrictions on travel bookings.

## FIN-003: Procurement and Spend Approval Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-003
**Version:** 2.4
**Effective date:** 1 September 2025
**Owner:** Procurement and Finance
**Applies to:** All purchases of goods and services made on behalf of XYZ Company

### 1. Principles

XYZ Company buys goods and services through a controlled process to achieve value for money, manage supplier risk, and protect against fraud. No commitment may be made to a supplier without an approved purchase order.

### 2. Purchase requisitions and quotes

2.1 A purchase requisition must be raised in the Procurement System for any spend above $1,000.

2.2 For purchases above $10,000, at least three written quotes must be obtained from suppliers. Where only one or two suppliers can meet the requirement, the requester must document the reason in the requisition.

2.3 Suppliers should be selected from the Preferred Supplier List where a suitable supplier exists on it.

### 3. Approval matrix

| Total purchase value | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| Up to $10,000 | Budget owner |
| $10,000.01 to $100,000 | Head of Department and Procurement Manager |
| Above $100,000 | Chief Financial Officer and the Procurement Committee |

Approvers must hold budget authority for the relevant cost centre. Splitting a purchase into smaller parts to avoid an approval threshold is a breach of this policy.

### 4. Contracts

4.1 Any contract with a total value above $250,000 requires review by the Legal team before signature.

4.2 Contracts that include personal data processing must also be reviewed by the Data Protection Officer, as described in COM-001.

4.3 Only authorised signatories listed in the Delegation of Authority register may sign contracts.

### 5. Purchase orders and payment

5.1 A valid purchase order must exist before a supplier invoice is accepted. Invoices received without a purchase order are returned to the supplier. This is known as the "No PO, No Pay" rule.

5.2 Standard supplier payment terms are net 45 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Early payment discounts may be taken where the Treasury team confirms the benefit.

### 6. Conflicts of interest

Employees involved in a purchasing decision must declare any personal or financial relationship with a supplier before the decision is made. Declarations are recorded in the Conflicts Register. Gifts and hospitality from suppliers are subject to COM-002.

### 7. Monitoring

Procurement performs quarterly spend analysis to identify maverick spend (purchases made outside approved suppliers or without a purchase order). Findings are reported to the CFO.
